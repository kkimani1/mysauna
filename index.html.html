The garden

A steam room built around what grows here

The Garden Herbal Sauna sits in a compound off Old Naivasha Road, sharing the same gate as Children's Garden Home and School — look for that name and you've found us.

Inside, sessions are built around three things: a wood-heated herbal sauna, gwasha treatment, and a plain steam bath. No frills, no long menus — just heat, herbs and time set aside for your body.

Come alone to sweat it out after a long week, or bring a friend. We keep the pace slow and the door open from morning to late evening.

What's on offer

Three ways to sweat

Book one on its own, or combine them into a single visit — tell us what you'd like when you book.

Herbal SaunaKSh 500

Herbal sauna

A wood-fired steam room where local herbs are added to the heat, meant to open you up and ease tired muscles and joints. Sessions run at a pace you set.

Traditional TherapyKSh 500

Gwasha

A traditional scraping and rubbing therapy with warm herbal application, done by hand. Popular for stiffness, poor circulation and general body pain.

Steam BathKSh 500

Steam

A plain, high-heat steam bath for anyone who wants the sweat without the herbs — a quicker option when you're short on time but still need to unwind.

Flat rate — KSh 500 per person, per session, for any of the three.

First time here

What a visit looks like

Same order every time, so there's nothing to guess when you arrive.

01

Arrive and settle

Get to the gate at Children's Garden Home and School on Old Naivasha Road — we're on the same compound. Come in, and we'll confirm your booked slot and service.

02

Change and prepare

Change into what's provided, and we'll walk you through what to expect for the specific session you've picked — sauna, gwasha or steam.

03

Your session

Time in the herbal sauna, on the gwasha table, or in the steam room. Go at your own pace — step out for water or air whenever you need to.

04

Rest before you leave

A few minutes to cool down and drink water before you head out. No rush to clear the room.
